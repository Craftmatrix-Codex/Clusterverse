# Code of Conduct

Clusterverse should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and treat people with respect.
- Give and receive feedback thoughtfully.
- Welcome questions, different perspectives, and first-time contributors.
- Protect privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Please report concerns privately through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Retaliation against good-faith reports is not acceptable.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
